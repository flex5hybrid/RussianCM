AbominationDeathSystem

When any abomination dies, gib them and seed a patch of flesh kudzu at their feet.

AbominationFleshKudzuSystem

Periodic heal-tick for abominations standing on a flesh kudzu tile, plus occasional sob/cry/scream emotes. Damage tick for non-abominations is handled by upstream DamageContacts on the kudzu prototype. Abomination melee attacks on tendons are rejected here so the threat can't trash its own coverage. Also drives the tiny everywhere-passive heal on every abomination (see AbominationComponent.PassiveHeal).

AbominationInfectionSystem

Abomination melee hits roll AbominationComponent.InfectionChance against each humanoid hit. Once infected the victim ramps from light coughs and drunkenness up to constant seizures and vomiting over CrescendoAfter minutes. Any infected death polymorphs the body into a mimic and seeds flesh kudzu at the corpse.

AbominationMimicSystem

Drives the mimic's transform action and disguise lifetime. Flow:

  1. Transform action -> picker BUI -> StartDisguise polymorphs into a real CMMobHuman, patches name/factions/IFF/skills/appearance, and grants the disguised entity an explicit Revert button.
  2. Disguise lasts TransformDuration (default 4.5min). Expiry, crit/death, and pressing the Revert button all funnel through .
  3. BeginRevert adds AbominationMimicRevertingComponent which spends a couple of seconds jittering + screaming, then polymorph-reverts the entity back into its combat form. The cooldown (TransformCooldown, default 5min) is stamped onto the original mimic at this point.

AbominationNestSpawnSystem

Global flesh-nest spawning. Every tick picks one random nest and spawns one non-mimic abomination at it. The base interval is 5 minutes with one nest, and each additional nest reduces the interval by 3 seconds, floored at 30 seconds.
