AU14NetSpliceSystem

CLF net splicing. A trained insurgent operator uses a splice kit on a mast or array, runs a do-after to get the feeder junction open, and then plays the band: each hidden trunk carrier has to be found by probing positions and reading the strength that comes back, then committed to with a lock. Probes are a fixed budget for the whole job and each one raises a detection meter, so it is a budget problem rather than a timer. Locking every carrier grafts the cell nets onto the anchor and leaves a tap behind. Running out of probes or filling the meter alarms the junction instead.

The mast sparks for the duration of the job, and the tap left by a successful one is a visible entity anyone can pull off (see AU14NetSpliceTapComponent).
