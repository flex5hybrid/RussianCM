Class GunSwitchComponent

The illegal "Switch" auto-sear chip (crafted at the Sapper's Workbench). While the chip is engaged (via its AttachableToggleable action, which reads as the gun's "Switch" fire mode) the host weapon fires absurdly fast, loses most of its accuracy, and every shot has a small chance to jam the gun (see GunJammedComponent).
