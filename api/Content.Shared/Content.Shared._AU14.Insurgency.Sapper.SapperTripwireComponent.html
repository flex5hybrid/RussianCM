Class SapperTripwireComponent

The "device" half of a two-part tripwire trap. When planted it strings a near-invisible wire forward from itself to an anchor a few tiles away (the "wire end"). Anything that isn't a friendly crossing any part of that wire sets the whole thing off.

The device itself carries no blast: its punch is entirely the explosives the sapper attaches to it (grenades, mines, C4 - anything with an Explosive component). Everything attached detonates together when the wire is crossed.

The device reuses SapperTrapComponent for its plant / arm / hide / disarm lifecycle; this component only adds the wire and the attached-payload behaviour on top.
