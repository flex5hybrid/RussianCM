CMU OSD Cyrillic

Based on UAV OSD Sans Mono by Nicholas Kruse.
Original source: https://www.dafont.com/uav-osd-sans.font
Author website: https://nicholaskruse.com
Original author's published usage statement:
"Font is free to use for personal or commercial use."
The source is listed as 100% Free. This records the author's statement;
it does not assign a different license to the original outlines.

Original bundled file: Resources/Fonts/UAVOSD/UAV-OSD-Sans-Mono.ttf
Original glyphs, advance widths and vertical metrics are preserved.
Original designer metadata is retained in the extended font.

Cyrillic extension: CMU contributors, 2026.
Adds the 33 Russian letters in both cases, Russian quotation marks,
numero sign, en/em dashes and ellipsis. Existing punctuation is unchanged.
Lowercase Russian maps to capital-shaped glyphs, matching the original
font's treatment of lowercase Latin.

Reproducible generator: Tools/cmu/fonts/extend_uav_osd.py
New family name: CMU OSD Cyrillic; version 1.001.
